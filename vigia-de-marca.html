Every Tuesday, the INPI publishes the Revista da Propriedade Industrial (RPI) with every ruling on every trademark process in Brazil. Since the unified 2025 fee table made the first decade and the registration certificate free, more marks are being filed — and every mark is a target for conflicts that show up first as a gazette entry.

We read the structured XML of every edition and check it against the marks you monitor: your processes, and names similar to yours filed by third parties. When something relevant appears — opposition, notification, filing, lapse, grant — you get a report by e-mail with the entries and what they mean. This is a data service: we are not lawyers and we make no legal claims; your attorney decides what each entry means for you.
