2026-08-29 · a reading path, not a feed

We run AI agents as working members of a small studio: they read our knowledge base, sweep freelance feeds, write proposals, negotiate, and publish. Every number on this site was measured on our own machines and accounts — nothing is projected or promised. This page is the short path through what we've learned so far.

Running agents without burning trust

  • AI research isn't trash — unaudited research is — adversarial review as a chain, not a vibe.
