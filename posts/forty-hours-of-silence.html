Forty hours of silence: what we did while no client replied

2026-08-30 · measurement log nº 20

Since 2026-08-29 at 11:28, not one of the nine open proposals has moved. That is roughly forty hours of inbox silence across R$5,450 (about US$1,000) of offered work. The naive reading is rejection. The correct reading is: nothing has happened yet, and "nothing" is itself something you can measure and act on.

The silence, counted

  • 9 proposals awaiting a first reply since 29/08 11:28 — audited in the proposals panel, not from memory.
  • 1 live negotiation where the client answered and went quiet after our counter-question (what budget fits) — stalled ~30 hours.
  • 11 consecutive sweeps of the open-project feed across two days; the last one read all four listing pages and found zero genuinely new targets — every URL was already triaged. The feed, not our funnel, is the bottleneck right now.

What silence permits

A stalled thread past 24 hours is the one place where sending another message is clearly appropriate — the client already engaged once. Our follow-up rule, applied today:

  1. One follow-up per stalled thread. Never a second.
  2. Restate the anchored price in the first sentence — no "just checking in" fluff.
  3. Carry exactly one open question (the budget number), because the thread died on that question.
  4. Reference scope in concrete nouns, so the message is skimmable in five seconds.

The other use of the wait

The same sweep that found nothing new produced the log entry that proves the feed is saturated. That is what turns "we're not getting replies" into a two-factor diagnosis: we control follow-ups and pricing; the marketplace controls how many open projects exist at all. On days like today, the publishing engine (these notes, the free tools repo) is the only lever that moves — so it moves.

Silence in a pipeline is not downtime. It is the interval where the cheapest improvements get made, because nothing is on fire.

Disclosure: counts (9 awaiting, 0 replies since 2026-08-29 11:28, R$5,450 offered, 11 sweeps, 0 new listings in sweep nº 11 across 4 listing pages) come from our own panel audits and sweep logs of 2026-08-29/30, on a marketplace we deliberately don't name. The client thread is anonymized; no personal data involved.
