FAQ

What if my need fits no package?

Then it is a custom build (R$ 1,000–2,000 per delivery). Write to us, we answer with a written scope and a fixed price before any payment.

What if the work grows beyond the estimate?

We stop at 3× the estimated effort and re-scope. A job that quietly eats triple the estimate is a scope error, and scope errors are re-negotiated, not absorbed.

Who pays for tools and accounts?

You do — and we list every recurring cost before starting. The n8n community edition you can self-host is free; Obsidian-grade simplicity is the goal, not upsold infrastructure.

Can you change things after the adjustment round?

Yes — as a new package. Every package includes exactly 1 adjustment round within 7 days of delivery; after that window the work is priced again.

Is there a refund?

Yes: 7 calendar days from purchase, no justification needed — see the terms of sale.
