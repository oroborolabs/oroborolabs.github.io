What the audit checks — 5 families

  1. Privacy policy — is there one, and does it actually cover the pages that collect data?
  2. Cookies — is there a notice, and which cookie categories does the site actually load?
  3. Forms — which forms collect data without a notice next to them?
  4. Trackers — known third-party trackers present on the pages.
  5. Security headers — which recommended headers are missing.

FAQ

Is this legal advice?

No. The audit applies documented technical heuristics to what is publicly visible on your site. It does not interpret the LGPD for your specific case and does not replace a lawyer. Anything legal-looking in the report is a pointer, not a conclusion.

Does the audit make my site compliant?

It shows the gaps and the order to fix them. We make no statement of compliance — that is exactly the promise we refuse to make.

What data do you collect about me?

The URL you send (for the demo or the audit) and what the payment processor needs to charge you. No name or e-mail is required to run the demo. See the privacy policy.
