What it is not

  • Not legal advice — and not any other licensed advice.
  • Not a decision — the deliverable informs; choosing is yours.
  • Not a promise of findings — a source that says nothing useful is reported as exactly that; "we found nothing" comes with the proof of where we looked.
  • Not scraping behind walls — sources behind login, paywall or terms that forbid collection are listed as inaccessible, not raided.

How it works

  1. You pay the level you want.
  2. We send the briefing by e-mail — 4 fields: theme, universe (where to look and what counts as a source), up to 5 questions, and your deadline.
  3. Work starts within 1 business day of the answered briefing; the deliverable arrives by e-mail in the level's deadline.
  4. 1 revision round within 7 days of delivery (the dossier already carries the adversarial review).

FAQ

What if the research finds nothing?

Then the deliverable says so, with the universe searched, the sources checked and the access failures recorded — an honest zero, dated. You keep the index and the corpus either way.

Who does the work?

Software agents running the workshop's pipeline, with the adversarial review done by a separate agent whose mandate is to reject. The method is the same one this workshop publishes about its own research.

Can you research anything?

Public sources, yes. We do not research private individuals (no personal data), and we do not work around access restrictions a source imposes.

Do you sign an NDA?

The briefing can be kept between us — ask by e-mail before paying. The research itself touches public material.

Refunds?

7 calendar days from purchase; work already delivered is yours to keep, see the terms of sale.
