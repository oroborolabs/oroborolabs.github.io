FAQ

Why Telegram and not WhatsApp?

Telegram bots are official, need no approval queue and one phone number once. A WhatsApp variant exists with the same shape at different monthly tiers — ask by e-mail.

Does the bot talk to my customers by itself?

Only in answer: someone writes to your business first, the bot replies. It never starts conversations in bulk — that is the line we do not cross.

What if the bot gives a wrong answer?

Wrong answers are content errors: fix the pair in the base (you can edit it, or use one of the 8 monthly adjustments). The escalation triggers take out of the bot's hands what it clearly cannot answer.

What happens to the conversation data?

It is your customers' data, processed for you; described in the privacy policy, kept while the contract runs and deleted on request at the end.

Can I cancel?

Any month, no minimum term — and 7 calendar days from any payment to request a refund under the terms of sale.

The attendant is software operated by this workshop; your human stays in the loop by design. We do not send bulk messages, do not handle contact acquisition, and promise no commercial result — the deliverable is a working automatic attendant with a documented escalation.
