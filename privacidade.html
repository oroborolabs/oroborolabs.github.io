What we do not do

  • No sale or sharing of data with advertisers; no analytics or tracking scripts on this site.
  • No newsletter — we do not keep a mailing list.
  • No research on private individuals — our research services work on public sources only.
  • No bulk messaging by the attendants we operate: they answer who writes first.

Who processes data for us

The payment processor (Stripe) and the e-mail provider — each under its own contract as a processor. Payment processing may involve international transfer; the processor's own privacy policy applies to that step.

How long we keep it

E-mails: while the exchange and any contracted service last, plus the time the law requires for accounting and tax duties. Audit inputs and reports: while the contract lasts and up to 1 year after, unless deletion is requested earlier. Attendant conversations: while the client's contract runs; deleted on request at its end.

Your rights

Confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, portability, deletion, information about sharing, and revocation of consent — the rights of art. 18 of the LGPD. Write to contato@oroborolabs.com and we answer. If you believe a right was violated, you may also file a complaint with the ANPD (Brazil's data protection authority).
